# Code of Conduct

Metadata Remover should be a practical, respectful place to work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but listen when someone says something caused harm.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the code, behavior, risks, and tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's files, metadata, contact details, or security report details without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information or sensitive files without consent.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
